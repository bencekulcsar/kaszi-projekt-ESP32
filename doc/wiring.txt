------        -----------

------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------

Signal        Connects to
------        -----------

GND           GND
SPK_1         Speaker +
SPK_2         Speaker -

------------------------------------------------------
NOTES
------------------------------------------------------
- Display and SD reader share the same physical SCK/MOSI
  bus (GPIO37/GPIO38), separated by their own CS lines
  (display CS = GPIO36, SD CS = GPIO39). Only one device
  is selected at a time, so this is safe.
- GPIO0, GPIO43, GPIO44, GPIO45 are intentionally left
  free (boot-strapping / USB-serial debug pins).
- All GND pins across all components must share a common
  ground with the ESP32 board.
